## 3. Right-bank vs left-bank features (verified rows)

Method caveat [P]: a "bank" is not a dialect. ЕУМ places the SW/SE boundary at the line «Фастів — Біла Церква — Ставище — Тальне — Первомайськ — Ананьїв» (um159), i.e. the Right-bank centre (Kyivshchyna, Cherkashchyna) belongs to the SOUTH-EASTERN dialect together with the Left bank. So the features below discriminate SW (Volhynia/Podillia/Galicia, always Right bank or further west) from SE/North (Left bank, and the Right-bank centre). "Weight" is the project's evidential strength (0-3), not a source figure.

Chronology gate [P]: Shevelov dates the SE complex «викшталтувані в другій половині XVI ст. та в XVII ст.» (ch. 43, «З мовного погляду, на відзисканих землях …»); ЕУМ um160: «П.-с. н. остаточно сформувалося у 19 — 20 ст.»; Sloboda and steppe «виникли як наслідок переселення і дозаселення людності протягом 16 — 18 ст.». So BK-SE-F>X, BK-SE-RSOFT, BK-SE-NOAFFR, BK-SE-VERB must not be applied to texts before 1700 and are strong only after 1798; BK-SW rows are usable from the early middle-Ukrainian period (SW texts of the 16th-17th c.).

Settlement frame for context [Q]: Shevelov ch. 43: «Нарешті, військовий тип осадництва переважав у степах: від південної околиці Києва й до Січі.»; the Medzhybizh 1717 settler tally is quoted there as a mixed-origin example.

## 4. Scoring model P(bank | cited features)

Evidence claim, not a fact [P-1.7]: the score ranks a text's *language* region and never overrides the witness's recorded region (kept as a separate claim).

1. Classes: R = Right bank (SW + Right-bank north), L = Left bank (SE, Siverian, East Polissian). Right-bank centre (Kyivshchyna) is flagged as `R/L ambiguous`; the dialect-boundary line is quoted in 3.
2. Each matched row i yields `s_i = sign * w_i * g_i(date)`; sign = +1 for L rows, -1 for R rows; `w_i` from the weight column (0 = ignored). `g_i(date)` = 0 if the text predates the feature's earliest dated period in the table, else 1 (rows with "-" dates default to 1 only for texts >= 1798).
3. `logit = prior + Σ s_i * c`, `P(L) = 1/(1+exp(-logit))`, prior 0 (no information). `c` = 0.8 (a first calibration constant, to be tuned on witnesses of known region; the number is the project's, not a source's).
4. Output: P(L), P(R) = 1 - P(L), the matched rows with source+quote+locator, and the count of rows that were date-gated off. If fewer than 2 rows with weight >= 2 match, report `insufficient evidence` instead of a number.
5. Scribal-norm guard: rows from Church Slavonic-influenced texts count at half weight (the ЕУМ/Shevelov phonetic features are spoken-language claims and appear unevenly in written norms); the editors' own caveat for Zynoviiv («Окремі фонетичні явища … зближують мову Климентія з мовою сучасних північних говорів») shows that a Left-bank scribe may be tagged by the model as "N/L" rather than "SE".
6. Test case: Zynoviiv's manuscript (1700-09) should score L on BK-N-HARDC and BK-SE-RSOFT-style soft/depalatalised р only if matched (the editors report диспалаталізація р, so BK-RR-HARD does not fire), and give zero on BK-N-LV, giving P(L) well above 0.5 and agreeing with the editors' «Лівобережна Україна, Чернігівщина».
