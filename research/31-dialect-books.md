## 0. How a row works

- A row = regex on the lower-cased, accent-free **spelling** of the form (`c.form`; `ў ґ ы ê ô` and the apostrophe variants are kept as features) + the dialect code + weight + a **[Q] from the ЕУМ article of that same dialect** (locator = `http://litopys.org.ua/ukrmova/um<N>.htm`). ЕУМ gives phonetic transcription (`д’éкувати`, `пуотік`); the spelling pattern is our mapping of it [P] to the way writers who use the dialect (Franko, Stefanyk, Hutsul / Lemko / Polissian authors, folklore recorders) spell it.
- Gate = the existing `dialectGate`: fires only on forms that VESUM does not list as normative, from the blanket 1798 or the row's dated `earliest`, never before the dialect group existed (`DIALECT_GROUP_FROM`, Shevelov). Six new group codes were added: HUT, SYAN (надсянський), PKB (покутсько-буковинський), VOL, SLB, STP (+ gates, §2.2).
- Weights are heuristics (0.3–0.6): a single marker is weak evidence, the detector noisy-ORs the markers that fire (research/05 §3.2). Rule weight is not itself a sourced fact; the *cite* is.
- Lexical dialectisms live in `core/dialect-lex.ts` (§3): one rule per word × dialect, cite = the dictionary / ЕУМ quote, fires on the exact non-VESUM form.

## 4. Open items

- **Polissian**: Лисенко 1974 and Аркушин 2000 are only sampled (preface examples / ~40 clean entries). The whole of Аркушин (13 000 headwords with village numbers) could feed a much larger WPL table; the OCR is clean enough to parse, but a bulk copy would exceed cite-only, so a selection policy (e.g. only words attested in our corpus and absent from VESUM) is needed first. «Говірки Чорнобильської зони. Тексти» and Климчук's records are not online in plain text; Никончук's atlases (Лексичний атлас правобережного Полісся, 1994) likewise.
- **Rusyn / Carpathian lexical lists** (jazyk.rueportal.eu, slovnicok.rusyny.org, rusyndictionary.com): reachable or not as listed in §1, but no gloss-bearing page could be fetched politely in one request; needs sitemap-driven sampling.
- **Грінченко region labels** (hrinchenko.com, r2u.org.ua): refused by robots.txt / bot policy; the data already in `data/work/hrinchenko1908.json` is the corpus witness, not the labels.
- **Dating**: only the Shevelov-dated families have `earliest`; the other rows keep the 1798 blanket (research/23 §0 rule: an undated feature must not be projected onto earlier texts).
- **Weights** were not calibrated against a gold set; see research/05 §3.2 for the scoring convention.
