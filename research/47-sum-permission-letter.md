**Subject: request for permission to use data of the «Dictionary of the Ukrainian Language» (SUM-11) and the 20-volume «Dictionary of the Ukrainian Language» (SUM-20) in a non-commercial open project**

Dear colleagues,

I develop BULP (ukr-lexicon), an open non-commercial historical-lexicographic project (github.com/dmytrozahor/BULP, licence CC BY-NC-SA). It collects Ukrainian vocabulary of all periods and shows, for each word, which dictionaries attest it and how its senses changed.

To give modern literary-language words an academic basis, I ask your permission to use:
1. the headwords of SUM-11 and SUM-20;
2. grammar, stress, sense numbers and usage labels (розм., заст., діал., перен., спец. etc.);
3. short glosses (at most 200 characters per sense), marked with the source and linked to the article on the official site;
4. optionally, and only if the rights holder agrees, the full definitions.

I undertake to name the source, authors and publisher beside every entry and link to the official electronic edition; to make no commercial use; to add no illustrative quotations without your consent; to publish the result under CC BY-NC-SA with attribution; and to remove the data promptly on request. Until you reply the project keeps only headwords and service data, limits glosses to 200 characters, and does not crawl your sites (the robots.txt of sum20ua.com forbids it, and I respect that).

If possible, I also ask for a data export (a file with headwords, senses, labels, and volume and page numbers), or for the terms on which one can be obtained.
