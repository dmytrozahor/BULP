## 2. Ingestion path ready for an open dump

If result pages (or the same markup) arrive under a licence, place them in `data/raw/r2u/` as `index.jsonl` (`{q, scope, file, status}` per line) + `pages/<file>.html.gz` and run `tsx pipelines/ingest.ts r2u_umanets` (etc.):

- `pipelines/r2u.ts` — parser of the portal's markup (span classes `KrymRusBold`, `KrymUkr`, `KrymUkrBold`, `KrymRusPlain`, `u.quote`, `num`); `R2U_DICTS` = the dictionaries to model.
- `pipelines/ingest-r2u.ts` — one `dictionary` witness per dictionary → `data/work/r2u_*.json`, plus `data/work/r2u-rows.json`.
- `pipelines/nw-r2u.ts` — units and authors (witness date = the printed edition's years, cited from the portal's list row; compilers as provisional authors, Грінченко reuses the vetted record).
- `pipelines/build-r2u.ts` → `public/data/r2u.json` (ru→uk pairs and `by[lemma]` = native equivalents of Russian headwords: evidence axis for surzhyk/calque claims; cite-only, quotes ≤ 300). Hook in `build.ts`; silent when no rows file exists.
- `core/orthography.ts` fold **ORTH-SYA** (eras `желехівка`, `r2u`): detached «ся» is joined as «-ся» by the parser (raw kept) and folded to `ся`; Желехівка ї/є folds still branch.
- `WitnessOut.noFreq` (`core/types.ts`, `build.ts`): attestation-only witnesses — they date spellings, can be a first attestation (weakest rank), give one KWIC line, but add nothing to N, f, ipm, dispersion, collocations, phrases or the keep rule, and no epoch. A lemma is kept because of r2u only if it is in the `queried` list.

Dictionaries (`R2U_DICTS`): Уманець–Спілка 1893–98 (9), Желехівський 1886 (21), Грінченко 1907–09 (12) and 3rd ed. 1927–28 (18), Ніковський 1927 (8), Голоскевич 1929 (11), Кримський–Єфремов 1924–33 (1), Ізюмов 1930 (2), Дорошенко 1930 (5), Якубські 1928 (13). Not modelled: phraseological, technical, modern, loanword dictionaries.

## 4. Limits

The parser is regex-based on span classes, developed against pages that have since been deleted, and covered only by the fixture; a real dump needs a fresh fixture check. Multi-word equivalents are dropped by design. No data was rebuilt or published (CX's rebuild pending): this is a code-only change.
