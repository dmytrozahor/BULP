# R42 — reflexes of ѣ (ять) in Ukrainian (SPEC 63 YT-1)

Fetched 2026-10-16 (ЕУМ and Шевельов over plain http, cp1251, `curl -k` because litopys.org.ua/izbornyk certificates do not match; uk.wikipedia via WebFetch). Every quote is verbatim from the fetched page (whitespace normalised, entities decoded). Rules built from it: `core/yat.ts`; counts: `analysis/yat-interchange.md`; the standard ѣ > і is R09 (`core/phonology.ts`) and is not repeated.

Dating (uk.wikipedia «Ять», secondary): in the south the sound became closed [e] in the first half of the 11th c.; narrowed to і in Galicia and Bukovyna by the late 13th c., in Volhynia no later than 1434; north: unstressed monophthongisation no later than the start of the 15th c. Group gates come from Shevelov ch. 8 (`DIALECT_GROUP_FROM`): NDN/LEM/BOY 1201, WPL 1301, ZAK 1500, MPL/SLB/STP 1501.

## 3. Is нігди supported?

Yes, as a labelled hypothesis, not as the standard continuant. Attestation: Кримський–Єфремов mark нігди «диал., зап.» (west), and Чубинський prints it (19th c.). Mechanism: ЕУМ «Ять» (unstressed ѣ gives е or и) and ЕУМ «Середньополіський» (unstressed final -ѣ ending gives -и). No source found that states «final -ѣ > -и in adverbs» as a rule by name; the rule YT-FIN-I is therefore limited to adverbs in -гдѣ (the only lexical class with a dated form) and carries all four citations. Corpus (analysis section): гди in Величковський (1680–91), гди in a Moldavian charter of 1458 and in two Sophia graffiti (1590–1690), нігди in Величковський; the same adverb is far more often written гдѣ/где/гді (counts by witness in the corpus, search over data/work).

## 5. Сакович (YT-3)

Text: izbornyk old17 page 12 (`http://izbornyk.org.ua/old17/old17_12.htm`), «Подається за публікацією у кн.: Українська поезія. Кінець XVI — початок XVII ст., с. 322 — 338». The editors modernised the orthography (Ђ → і/ї/и), so the unit is a lexical/morphological witness (`orth: 'modernised'`) and is excluded from the ѣ/и interchange counts. Witness unit `i17_12` added (pipelines/nw-izb17.ts); author vetting line: «Зоставивши теди нам святиню в Росії». The 1622 print is public domain; an original-spelling transcription was not found on izbornyk or elsewhere (UKMA PDF is an unreadable image).
