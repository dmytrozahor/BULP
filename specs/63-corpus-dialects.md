## AC — automatic classification (user 2026-10-14: *"You ought not to manually classify everything it should be automatic"*)
Labels (dialect, region, surzhyk/contact, usage/register, archaism, POS) are produced by code from what the sources already say, never by hand-curated word lists. Hand lists survive only as test fixtures and seeds for evaluation.
- **AC-1** Label extraction: every ingested dictionary witness (Грінченко, Уманець–Спілка, Ніковський, ЕУМ, dialect dictionaries, uk.wiktionary, r2u) is parsed for its own label abbreviations («діал.», «розм.», «заст.», «рідко», «пол.», «рос.», «гал.», «волин.», region names…) through one abbreviation table per source (taken from the source's list of abbreviations, cited) → claims {label, source, verbatim quote, locator}. No label without such a quote.
- **AC-2** Region → dialect group mapping is computed from a cited gazetteer (research/04 areas; place/region name → group), not assigned per word.
- **AC-3** Surzhyk/contact: computed from r2u Russian→Ukrainian pairs + VESUM/pymorphy3-ru membership (form ∉ VESUM ∧ ∈ ru dictionary ∧ r2u gives a Ukrainian equivalent) with the r2u quote; no manual lists.
- **AC-4** Phonetic dialect markers stay rule-based (DIALECT_RULES) but rule rows are generated from the dialectology sources' feature tables where machine-readable; every lemma's labels are recomputed on each build.
- **AC-5** Report: per label type coverage before/after, precision on the hand fixtures (old manual lists), in analysis/auto-classification.md.

## SE — sense evolution: how a meaning changed across the epochs, as the dictionaries treat it (owner 2026-10-17, repeated 2026-10-21: *«нам треба формальний флоу за евіденсами переходження значень слів як їх трактують словники»* · *«у кожній картці послідовно має бути видно як змінювався сенс залежно від епохи»* · *"it is important to get the dictionaries with word meanings over epochs and years and make it in each card"*; goal: beat goroh.pp.ua in data quality; prefix SE = sense evolution, unused before; builds on SN, which only attaches dated senses)
SN dates a sense by the print year of the dictionary line. A historical dictionary describes an older language than its print year, and a transition between senses was never modelled. SE adds both, with the rigour the sound rules already have («no cite, no rule» becomes «no cite, no transition»). Pure model: `core/se.ts`; build stage `senses`: `pipelines/build-se.ts`; card block: `src/components/SenseEpochs.vue`.
- **SE-1** (sense inventory) Per lemma, every dictionary that carries a sense line gives one **row**: dictionary id, gloss (`''` = the source attests the word but its sense text is not kept), part of speech, verbatim quote ≤ 200 characters with the locator inside the dictionary, and the dictionary's own type labels (SE-4). Each dictionary has a fixed record (`DICT_COVER` in core/se.ts): print **edition years** and its **coverage period** with the basis `period` (a historical dictionary describes an earlier period than its print year), `own-time` (it describes the language of its edition years) or `modern` (current usage, the source does not date its senses). Declared periods: Словник староукраїнської мови XIV–XV ст. (1977–78) covers 1301–1500; Словник української мови XVI – першої половини XVII ст. 1500–1650; Тимченко «Історичний словник українського язика» (1930–32) the 16th–18th-c. quotations (1501–1800); Желехівський 1886, Уманець–Спілка 1893–98, Грінченко 1907–09 (3rd ed. 1927–28), Кримський–Єфремов 1924–33, Ніковський 1927 and the other r2u dictionaries describe their own time (edition years); СУМ-11 1970–80 is modern; uk.wiktionary and en.wiktionary (kaikki) are `modern` with **no** date (the retrieval year is never a date of a sense). **A dated quotation outranks the period**: where a dictionary quotes a dated source for a sense, the date of that quotation is the row's attestation date (`e`, basis `quote`); the dictionary witness of the corpus is split by quotation decade (`dsu14_<vol>_<decade>`), a `_nd` row falls back to the coverage period. Rows come from (a) every token of a dictionary witness linked to a lemma, a light card, a dictionary-only card or an /ar card (the SN-1 gloss reader `glossOf`; Желехівський is read like a uk→ru article; for the XIV–XV ст. dictionary only the headword line is kept in the corpus, so a gloss exists only where the headline has text after the part-of-speech mark, the rest is the sense text of a copyrighted work and is not copied), (b) uk.wiktionary senses (BL-9 parser, CC BY-SA 4.0), (c) en.wiktionary senses of the kaikki dump (form-of / alt-of senses are no senses). ≤ 6 rows per lemma and source, ≤ 20 per lemma.
- **SE-2** (alignment) Rows are grouped into one **sense** when their glosses are in the same language and similar (Dice coefficient of the stems of the gloss words, identical first terms = 1, threshold `MATCH_MIN` = 0.5) or when two dictionaries share a quotation (three consecutive words, score 1, `why: quote`). Each group carries the match score (mean of its matched pairs) and `link: 'inferred'`; `link: 'xref'` is set only when a dictionary cross-references the other (no cross-reference parser exists yet, so every current match is «inferred»). Rows without a gloss of one dictionary form one group «слово засвідчене, текст значення не взято». **Limit, stated:** glosses in different languages are never matched (no translation is guessed), so a Russian gloss of Грінченко and a Ukrainian gloss of Вікісловник stay two senses until a cross-reference joins them.
- **SE-3** (corpus attestation per sense) A dated corpus context (KWIC of the card, dictionary witnesses excluded) is assigned to a sense only by evidence: (a) three consecutive words of the context stand in the quotation of a row of that sense (`via: quote`), or (b) a collocation cue named in the gloss' parenthesis occurs next to the node, in exactly one sense (`via: cue`). Everything else, and every ambiguous case, is **unassigned** and only counted per epoch. A lemma with one sense does not make its contexts that sense's (never guess). ≤ 3 assigned contexts per sense.
- **SE-4** (transitions) A transition **A → B** is created only between senses attested in different epochs, and only when B has exactly one candidate predecessor in the closest earlier attested epoch (several candidates = no arrow, the origin is not guessed). It is **typed** (`figurative`, `metaphor`, `metonymy`, `narrowing`, `broadening`, `pejoration`, `amelioration`, `calque`) only when a row of B carries a label that names the type (`TYPE_LABELS`: «перен.», «зневажл.», en.wiktionary tags «figuratively», «pejorative», the word «метафора» …); the label is quoted. Otherwise the transition reads «зміна значення (тип не вказано джерелом)». Status: **cited** = a source names the type; **inferred** = both ends are dated by dictionary quotations, no type named; **hypothesis** = at least one end rests on a coverage period or edition year only (the order may only mirror which dictionary covers which period). A sense that stops being attested creates nothing (a gap is not a loss of the meaning). Glosses of different languages are never compared, so no transition is created between them. A transition is only as good as the alignment (SE-2): the status says how far to trust it.
- **SE-5** (card block) Every card shows **«Значення за епохами»**: a sequential timeline over the project's six Shevelov epochs (`shev.*`, the same as «клітинки за епохами»). Each epoch lists its senses with the dictionary (print years and coverage period), the gloss, the dated quotes and the corpus contexts assigned by SE-3; arrows between epochs show the transitions with type or «тип не вказано джерелом» and the status. An epoch with no evidence is a **gap** («немає даних»), never filled; a source whose period spans several epochs (e.g. 1301–1500) is shown in each of them with «?»; a modern source that does not date its senses sits in the last epoch marked «не датує значення». Unassigned corpus contexts are shown as a count per epoch.
- **SE-6** (card kinds) The block is mounted on every card kind: full (/w), light and dictionary-only (/lw), ORV/TOROT (/orv, /name), archaic (/ar), unverified / Middle-Ukrainian clusters (/uv). One self-contained component, one mount line per view; the data key is `lk(word)`.
- **SE-7** (build) `npm run build:data` runs the stage `senses` after `phrases` (RS-2 stage cache, key and read set as for the other stages; reads data/raw/wikt, data/raw/kaikki/uk.jsonl and the dictionary witnesses); output `data/work/se.json` (not committed), report line `SE: …` with the counts per dictionary, the aligned senses, the transitions and the typed ones.
- **SE-8** (delivery) `build-parts.ts` shards it into `lm/se/<bucket>.json` (`SE_BUCKETS` = 4096, key `lk(word)`; CDN dir `lm`); the shard is **lazy**: fetched only when the panel opens (`Panel` mounts its body on first open), so it is not in `cardData()` and the first-load and card budgets (PF-2) do not change. A missing shard reads as «немає даних про значення».
- **SE-9** (new dictionaries: policy) A dictionary joins the inventory by a harvester that writes the corpus' ordinary dictionary witness (headword + locator of the article line, resumable, atomic writes, RS-1) and one record in `DICT_COVER` (edition, coverage, basis, gloss language). Public domain or a free licence: the harvester may keep glosses. Copyrighted: only the headword line and **short quotes** (≤ 200 characters) of a sense with the source and locator; the rest is not copied. Not online: the book goes to the top of `research/45-literature-wanted.md` as an owner-needed book with its volume list. The decision per dictionary is in SE-10.

- **SE-11** (parsing the OCR of the historical dictionaries into dated sense rows; owner coordinator 2026-10-21; `core/se-parse.ts`, `pipelines/parse-se-dicts.ts`, `npm run parse:se`) One article parser per dictionary (`PARSE_SPECS`): Тимченко 1930/32 (`tymch1930`, `1.2.1932`; **only А–Жял was printed**, the coverage record says so), Білецький-Носенко (`slovnyk33`), Закревський (`bandurista3`), СУМ XVI–XVII (`SUM-28`, `SUM-28_2`), Матеріали XV–XVIII ст. (`kniga1`, `kniga2`), Словник мови Шевченка (`1_2023…`, `2_2023…`). An article = an upper-case headword with variants (Білецький-Носенко: a capitalised word + its grammar mark «(и. с. ж. р.)», Закревський: a capitalised word + comma), the grammar mark, the numbered senses with glosses, and the quotations with their sigla. A row carries the sense number (`n`), the gloss, the part of speech, the sigla (`s`), the headword as locator and, for a public-domain dictionary, ≤ 120 characters of the first quotation (`q`).
  (a) **Dating, in this order**: (1) a four-digit year inside the quotation («Краків, 1588», «Кн.Луцк. 1562»; СУМ XVI–XVII prints one in parentheses after almost every quotation); (2) the dictionary's century label («ХУП.» is the OCR of «XVII.» in Тимченко, so 1601–1700); (3) a siglum resolved through the dictionary's own list of sources, only where that list gives a year (`siglaDates`; empty for most sigla because the lists print no year); (4) otherwise no date: the row falls back to the coverage period of the dictionary, which the card labels «період» and marks «?» where it spans epochs. Dates of one sense are bucketed by Shevelov epoch: a sense quoted in the 15th and the 18th century is two rows in two epochs, never one row spanning the epochs between (no gap is filled). The Шевченко dictionary cites volume and line («І 113.1293»); the date of a Kobzar work is not looked up, the row sits in the coverage period 1837–1861 (the modern epoch).
  (b) **OCR quality gate (SE-11b)**: the project's OQ gate (SPEC 63 OQ, MM-5 agent) was not on origin/mvp when this was written; `ocrScore` / `headwordOk` / `glossOk` in core/se-parse.ts are the gate used here and are to be replaced by the OQ functions when they land. `ocrScore` = the share of the alphabetic tokens that are plausible Cyrillic words (a vowel, no run of six consonants, no Latin/digit mixing; runs of one-letter fragments count as debris). A page below 0.5 is skipped whole; an article below its dictionary threshold (0.72–0.85), with a headword that is not a word (century labels, Roman numerals, fewer than 3 letters), or whose gloss is garbled (two upper-case words in a row, a year, score < 0.8) is **skipped and counted, never emitted**. A headword is only a key: the build attaches a row to an existing card (exact key, else the first derivation candidate, flagged `m: 'd'` and shown «за правилами виведення»), it never creates a lemma from an OCR line. Cross-references («Див. X», «См. X») are counted apart and left out of the pass rate: pass rate = passed / (candidates − cross-references).
  (c) **Copyright**: for the © dictionaries (СУМ XVI–XVII, Матеріали, Словник мови Шевченка) a row holds only the headword, the sense number, a gloss ≤ 200 characters, the sigla and the quotation dates, no quotation text (`keepQuotes: false`). **Licence reasoning, owner to confirm**: СУМ XVI–XVII is a collective work of the Інститут українознавства НАН України (volumes 1994–2022, no personal author who died more than 70 years ago); «Матеріали» (2002–2003) is an edition by В. Німчук and Г. Лиса of the papers of Є. Тимченко † 1948 (the text is out of copyright, the editors' apparatus is not); Словник мови Шевченка (1964) is a collective work of the Інститут мовознавства. Under the Ukrainian term of life + 70 years none of the three is shown to be free, so only short glosses with the source are kept. Open question to the owner in research/45 (question Q-SE1): may we store more (full definitions, quotation text)? Public domain: Тимченко (Є. Тимченко † 1948), Білецький-Носенко († 1856), Закревський († 1871); the 1966 apparatus of Німчук is not taken.
  (d) **Where it runs, resumable (RS-1)**: the OCR lives on the harvester box (`data/raw/harvest/se-dicts`, written by harvest/se-dicts.ts) and, once synced like the other harvests, on the worker. The parse is pure CPU (seconds per dictionary), so it runs in both places: as the last step of `harvest/se-chain.sh` on the box, and on the worker in the ingest step (`npm run parse:se`). One output per archive item, `data/work/se-parsed/<id>.json`, written tmp + rename; an output whose `srcBytes` equals the current OCR size is reused, a torn index line or output is ignored; a missing item is skipped. The build stage `senses` reads `data/work/se-parsed` (read set tracked by the stage cache, so a new parse invalidates only `senses`). Report: `analysis/se-parse.md` (pass rate per dictionary, rows per epoch dated by a quotation versus by the period only).

## OQ — OCR quality gate (owner 2026-10-21, MM-8: first attestation 1092 from «Архангельське Євангеліє 1092 (OCR)», archive.org, not proofread; prefix OQ = OCR quality, unused before)
- **OQ-1** (implemented, effective next build:data) `core/ocr-quality.ts`: per-witness score S = L × (1 − H), L = share of tokens linked to a VESUM lemma by the build, H = hapax-type rate (word types seen once ÷ tokens). Junk-glyph tokens (Latin / digits inside Cyrillic) are already dropped by the tokenizer (measured 0.000 on the 7 pre-1700 OCR witnesses), so the link share and the hapax rate carry the signal. Measured on the live build (945 OCR witnesses with ≥ 1 500 tokens): median S of OCR < 1700 = 0.34, proofread < 1700 = 0.43; OCR 1700–1850 = 0.29, proofread 0.55; OCR ≥ 1850 = 0.55, proofread 0.67. Bands (OCR witnesses only): `garbage` S < 0.15; `low` S < 0.45 for date < 1800, S < 0.30 otherwise; else `ok`. The thresholds are ours (stated in the module), not from a source.
- **OQ-2** (implemented) effect of a gated witness (`low`, `garbage`, or an OCR witness that has a proofread edition in the corpus): it is never a first-attestation candidate (`firstByW`), never sets a form's oldest date (a date from gated OCR is replaced by the first real attestation even if later), contributes no epoch / century count (`perEpoch`, `perCentury`, `fa.epochs`) and no `pre1400` flag; it stays in frequencies and KWIC with the badge «OCR низької якості» (`Kwic.vue`; `WitnessMeta.oq`); a `garbage` witness is not even a KWIC line.
- **OQ-3** (implemented) preferred edition: `PROOFREAD_EDITION` maps OCR witness → proofread witness of the same work already in the corpus (matched by title and date): hv_ia_arch1092 → arch (TOROT), hv_ia_hypatian_codex → kievchr, gvl, hv_ia_gramat0slovenska → hv_ws_68448, hv_ia_enejida_1798 / hv_ia_eneyida1798 → hv_ws_23660, hv_ia_hrammatyka1818 → hv_ws_40481, hv_ia_hrammatyka1822 → hv_ws_41318 (izbornyk: no OCR duplicate found).
