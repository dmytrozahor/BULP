## AC — automatic classification (user 2026-10-14: *"You ought not to manually classify everything it should be automatic"*)
Labels (dialect, region, surzhyk/contact, usage/register, archaism, POS) are produced by code from what the sources already say, never by hand-curated word lists. Hand lists survive only as test fixtures and seeds for evaluation.
- **AC-1** Label extraction: every ingested dictionary witness (Грінченко, Уманець–Спілка, Ніковський, ЕУМ, dialect dictionaries, uk.wiktionary, r2u) is parsed for its own label abbreviations («діал.», «розм.», «заст.», «рідко», «пол.», «рос.», «гал.», «волин.», region names…) through one abbreviation table per source (taken from the source's list of abbreviations, cited) → claims {label, source, verbatim quote, locator}. No label without such a quote.
- **AC-2** Region → dialect group mapping is computed from a cited gazetteer (research/04 areas; place/region name → group), not assigned per word.
- **AC-3** Surzhyk/contact: computed from r2u Russian→Ukrainian pairs + VESUM/pymorphy3-ru membership (form ∉ VESUM ∧ ∈ ru dictionary ∧ r2u gives a Ukrainian equivalent) with the r2u quote; no manual lists.
- **AC-4** Phonetic dialect markers stay rule-based (DIALECT_RULES) but rule rows are generated from the dialectology sources' feature tables where machine-readable; every lemma's labels are recomputed on each build.
- **AC-5** Report: per label type coverage before/after, precision on the hand fixtures (old manual lists), in analysis/auto-classification.md.

## SE — sense evolution: how a meaning changed across the epochs, as the dictionaries treat it (owner 2026-10-17, repeated 2026-10-21: *«нам треба формальний флоу за евіденсами переходження значень слів як їх трактують словники»* · *«у кожній картці послідовно має бути видно як змінювався сенс залежно від епохи»* · *"it is important to get the dictionaries with word meanings over epochs and years and make it in each card"*; goal: beat goroh.pp.ua in data quality; prefix SE = sense evolution, unused before; builds on SN, which only attaches dated senses)
SN dates a sense by the print year of the dictionary line. A historical dictionary describes an older language than its print year, and a transition between senses was never modelled. SE adds both, with the rigour the sound rules already have («no cite, no rule» becomes «no cite, no transition»). Pure model: `core/se.ts`; build stage `senses`: `pipelines/build-se.ts`; card block: `src/components/SenseEpochs.vue`.
- **SE-1** (sense inventory) Per lemma, every dictionary that carries a sense line gives one **row**: dictionary id, gloss (`''` = the source attests the word but its sense text is not kept), part of speech, verbatim quote ≤ 200 characters with the locator inside the dictionary, and the dictionary's own type labels (SE-4). Each dictionary has a fixed record (`DICT_COVER` in core/se.ts): print **edition years** and its **coverage period** with the basis `period` (a historical dictionary describes an earlier period than its print year), `own-time` (it describes the language of its edition years) or `modern` (current usage, the source does not date its senses). Declared periods: Словник староукраїнської мови XIV–XV ст. (1977–78) covers 1301–1500; Словник української мови XVI – першої половини XVII ст. 1500–1650; Тимченко «Історичний словник українського язика» (1930–32) the 16th–18th-c. quotations (1501–1800); Желехівський 1886, Уманець–Спілка 1893–98, Грінченко 1907–09 (3rd ed. 1927–28), Кримський–Єфремов 1924–33, Ніковський 1927 and the other r2u dictionaries describe their own time (edition years); СУМ-11 1970–80 is modern; uk.wiktionary and en.wiktionary (kaikki) are `modern` with **no** date (the retrieval year is never a date of a sense). **A dated quotation outranks the period**: where a dictionary quotes a dated source for a sense, the date of that quotation is the row's attestation date (`e`, basis `quote`); the dictionary witness of the corpus is split by quotation decade (`dsu14_<vol>_<decade>`), a `_nd` row falls back to the coverage period. Rows come from (a) every token of a dictionary witness linked to a lemma, a light card, a dictionary-only card or an /ar card (the SN-1 gloss reader `glossOf`; Желехівський is read like a uk→ru article; for the XIV–XV ст. dictionary only the headword line is kept in the corpus, so a gloss exists only where the headline has text after the part-of-speech mark, the rest is the sense text of a copyrighted work and is not copied), (b) uk.wiktionary senses (BL-9 parser, CC BY-SA 4.0), (c) en.wiktionary senses of the kaikki dump (form-of / alt-of senses are no senses). ≤ 6 rows per lemma and source, ≤ 20 per lemma.
- **SE-2** (alignment) Rows are grouped into one **sense** when their glosses are in the same language and similar (Dice coefficient of the stems of the gloss words, identical first terms = 1, threshold `MATCH_MIN` = 0.5) or when two dictionaries share a quotation (three consecutive words, score 1, `why: quote`). Each group carries the match score (mean of its matched pairs) and `link: 'inferred'`; `link: 'xref'` is set only when a dictionary cross-references the other (no cross-reference parser exists yet, so every current match is «inferred»). Rows without a gloss of one dictionary form one group «слово засвідчене, текст значення не взято». **Limit, stated:** glosses in different languages are never matched (no translation is guessed), so a Russian gloss of Грінченко and a Ukrainian gloss of Вікісловник stay two senses until a cross-reference joins them.
- **SE-3** (corpus attestation per sense) A dated corpus context (KWIC of the card, dictionary witnesses excluded) is assigned to a sense only by evidence: (a) three consecutive words of the context stand in the quotation of a row of that sense (`via: quote`), or (b) a collocation cue named in the gloss' parenthesis occurs next to the node, in exactly one sense (`via: cue`). Everything else, and every ambiguous case, is **unassigned** and only counted per epoch. A lemma with one sense does not make its contexts that sense's (never guess). ≤ 3 assigned contexts per sense.
- **SE-4** (transitions) A transition **A → B** is created only between senses attested in different epochs, and only when B has exactly one candidate predecessor in the closest earlier attested epoch (several candidates = no arrow, the origin is not guessed). It is **typed** (`figurative`, `metaphor`, `metonymy`, `narrowing`, `broadening`, `pejoration`, `amelioration`, `calque`) only when a row of B carries a label that names the type (`TYPE_LABELS`: «перен.», «зневажл.», en.wiktionary tags «figuratively», «pejorative», the word «метафора» …); the label is quoted. Otherwise the transition reads «зміна значення (тип не вказано джерелом)». Status: **cited** = a source names the type; **inferred** = both ends are dated by dictionary quotations, no type named; **hypothesis** = at least one end rests on a coverage period or edition year only (the order may only mirror which dictionary covers which period). A sense that stops being attested creates nothing (a gap is not a loss of the meaning). Glosses of different languages are never compared, so no transition is created between them. A transition is only as good as the alignment (SE-2): the status says how far to trust it.
- **SE-5** (card block) Every card shows **«Значення за епохами»**: a sequential timeline over the project's six Shevelov epochs (`shev.*`, the same as «клітинки за епохами»). ## IN — incremental pipeline (owner 2026-10-22: *«оптимізуй скрипт аби ми працювали на вже імпортованих даних і додавали згори те що не має перетинів. + кешування стадій»*; every recent build was cold: a code change in one helper changed the fingerprint of nearly all stages, the ingest re-read whole sources, and a 6.5 GB CDN prefix was re-uploaded)
- **IN-1** Incremental ingest. Each item-level source (wikisource-uk, archive-*, hv5, hv5-orig) keeps a content manifest `data/work/ingest-manifest/<source>.json` (item id → `sig` = hash of what the harvester index states about the item, plus the witness id, URL, content hash and edition rank; the parameter string `gate1` invalidates the source when the OCR/language-gate rules change). A rerun processes only NEW or CHANGED items and appends their witness files to the existing ones; an item whose sig is unchanged and whose work file exists is never opened (a wikisource bundle whose items are all unchanged is not opened either); an item that vanished from the index is DELETED explicitly (its work file removed, one log line); a `--limit` run deletes nothing. Vocabulary-only sources are one aggregate each: unchanged index + parameters + existing output = skipped. `--full` ignores the manifests. Manifests are written atomically after the work files (RS-1).
- **IN-2** Overlap. A new witness whose id, URL or token-stream hash equals a witness of ANOTHER source (hv4 vs hv5, wikisource vs archive.org, …) is not written twice. Precedence: the vetted edition wins (rank scholarly_ed 4 > later_ed 3 > other 2 > ocr 1); equal rank keeps the existing one; a better newcomer supersedes the worse witness (its file is removed, its manifest entry marked `superseded`). Every decision is a log line. Ingesters outside ingest-harvest.ts (ingest-izbornyk, ingest-pum, …) are not yet behind this registry.
- **IN-3** Early cut-off. The RS-2 spine advances with the digest of a stage's OUTPUT (the value frames of its cache entry), not with its key: a stage recomputed to an identical result (a rule fix that changes nothing) leaves every later stage cached. A stage whose output could not be digested falls back to its key. The finish line logs `hit rate: X/Y stages (stage hit|miss Ns, …)`; per-witness stages log `N entries reused, M computed`.
- **IN-4** `build-parts` compares the staged outputs with the published ones byte for byte before the (still atomic) swap and logs `parts: N files, C changed, A new, R removed, I identical` (+ `data/work/parts-changed.json`).
- **IN-5** `data:unlinked` records per text file what it contributes (the unknown tokens and the two UV flags) in `data/work/unlinked-cache/`, keyed by the file's bytes and the hash of the lexicon core, and replays it for unchanged files; the OCR flag is re-read from the corpus at every replay; entries of vanished files are removed. Output is identical to a full scan (test).
- **IN-6** Symbol-level code fingerprints (`pipelines/symbol-graph.ts`). A stage is keyed by the declarations it can reach, not by the whole text of every module in its import closure: names the stage block and the glue it reaches mention → the exporting declaration → the same-module declarations and imports its text mentions (by name, conservative) → recursively. Module top-level statements that are not declarations, `import * as`, `export *`, side-effect and dynamic imports, non-TypeScript files are taken whole; types are erased. Editing `ocrScore` leaves `tokens` and `link` cached (it reaches `archaic` and later only); editing an unreached function changes no key.
- **IN-7** Delta CDN upload (`pipelines/data-cdn-delta.ts`). The manifest of a build is format 2 with `objects: {dir/rel: [sha1, stored size, big?]}`. `data-cdn.ts upload` hashes the local files, reads the base build's manifest (the live pointer, `--base ID`), copies every unchanged object INSIDE S3 into the new immutable prefix (`aws s3 sync s3://…/<base>/<dir> s3://…/<new>/<dir>` with the changed/deleted files excluded; unchanged gzipped big files in their own call with `--content-encoding gzip`, then verified with head-object), and uploads only new and changed files; deleted files are not carried over. Prefixes stay immutable and verified complete. A dir with more than 1500 changed/deleted files, a glob character in a name, a dir absent from the base, or a base without a format-2 manifest uploads whole (the PF-7 path). `--full` disables the delta. Not yet exercised against real S3 (fake bucket in the tests): the first delta upload should be watched.
- **IN-8** `ops/worker/ingest.sh` and `chain.sh` use the incremental paths by default; `--full` forces a full rebuild (ingest ignores markers and manifests; chain runs `build:data --no-cache`, clears the unlinked cache, uploads everything). Fixture benchmark (tests/incremental.test.ts): 200 witnesses, one edited = 1 computed, 199 reused; a 204-object upload with one changed shard sends exactly that shard and copies the rest in S3; a rerun with no data change sends nothing.
  *Estimate for the real corpus (NOT measured; from RS-2: cold ~2800 s, all-hit warm 1114 s, 6.5 GB prefix):* a build with no change ≈ 1114 s + 0 B uploaded. A one-rule fix inside `annotate` only ≈ 1114 s + the recompute of that stage and of the later ones whose output changes (≤ 2800 s worst case); the upload is the shards the rule touches (a lemma-hash-sharded dir changes in proportion to the touched lemmas; a typical fix: tens of MB to ~1 GB). One new book: its `tokens` entry is computed alone, but `link` and everything after it read every witness file and still recompute (≈ cold minus tokenisation, ~2500 s) until the open item below is done; upload: its text/witness files (1–5 MB) plus the index/lemma shards its words fall in, likely 0.5–2 GB of 6.5 GB, the rest copied in S3.
  *Open (not implemented):* per-witness / per-type / per-lemma memoisation inside `link`, `archaic`, `annotate`, `epochs` and `senses` (their cross-witness aggregates — lemma frequency, POS agreement, first attestation — need a delta merge, not a replay); until then a new witness recomputes those stages.

- **LB-11** (implemented, 2026-10-28; owner: «прокласифікуй усі слова за діалектною приналежністю, не має бути жодного без діалектної приналежності. скористайся для цього нашими матеріалами») **Exhaustive but honest classification.** Every entry gets exactly one of seven classes, in this order, each with an **evidence grade** (сильний / середній / слабкий; `gradeOf`, derived from the stored result, shown on the card, the Огляд line and the tooltip, and on `/labels-method`):

  3. **загальновживане за корпусом** (сильний): dispersion over ≥ 3 regions (LB-1 c, unchanged).
  4. **літературна норма за словником** (середній): a СУМ-11/20 headword (LB-9, now whenever 1–3 do not apply; the corpus rows are kept and shown) or a VESUM headword: «нормативне (ВЕСУМ), регіональних даних немає» (`sub = 'vesum'`; VESUM has no territorial tag, `dict_uk/doc/tags.txt`).   5. **засвідчене лише в пам’ятках регіону X** (слабкий): `WitSummary` (count of witnesses, regions, years): «засвідчено лише в N пам’ятках з X, XII ст.   7. **невизначено** (no grade) only when 1–6 do not apply, with the reason on the card (`why`: which dictionaries are the only sources and that their prefaces name no base, or that the witnesses have no established region); counted in the coverage report with the reasons.
